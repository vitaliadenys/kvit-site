Terms of Service

Kvit Reply Assistant · Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. The app

Kvit Reply Assistant is an internal tool of KVIT studio, operated by Vitaliia Olehivna Denys (FOP Denys Vitaliia Olehivna), sole proprietor. It helps studio staff find public Threads posts where people look for yoga, Pilates or movement classes in Ivano-Frankivsk, and reply to them from the @kvit.studio account after manual approval.

2. Who may use it

Only authorised Kvit Studio staff. The app is not offered to the public and does not allow anyone else to sign in.

3. Rules we follow

  • Every reply is reviewed and approved by a person before it is published.
  • Replies are relevant to the question asked, honest about who we are, and never sent in bulk or as repeated identical messages.
  • We do not reply to posts that appear to be written by minors, and we do not reply twice to the same person about the same request.
  • We comply with the Threads Terms of Use, Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

4. Data

How we handle data is described in our Privacy Policy. Deletion requests: Data Deletion.

5. Liability

Replies published by @kvit.studio reflect the views of KVIT studio. The app is provided as an internal tool, without warranties to third parties, to the extent permitted by Ukrainian law.
